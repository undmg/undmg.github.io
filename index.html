About Me

Hey, I'm Undmg — a developer who builds tools, games, and whatever else catches my interest. I work with Roblox/Lua, Python, and web technologies. Most of my projects are born from "I wish this existed" moments.

I'm also big on AI-assisted development. I use tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot daily — not as a crutch, but as a multiplier. Knowing how to write effective prompts, break down complex problems, and guide AI to produce clean, working code is a skill I've refined over time. It's basically prompt engineering meets real development.

My approach is simple: build fast, iterate faster. I care more about shipping something useful than making it perfect on the first try. Most of my tools — like RBXL to Text Tool and various other useful tools — came from solving my own problems and then sharing them with others.

When I'm not coding, I'm probably browsing dev tools, messing with new AI models, or procrastinating on Discord. If you've got a project idea or just want to chat, feel free to reach out.
