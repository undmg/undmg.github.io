§ 01 — AboutUNDMG Issue 04p. 02

Hi. I'm Undmg.
I make things
people play & use.

I make games on Roblox, tools that save developers time, and web pages that feel more like games than websites. Most of it starts with a feeling I want back: how a car slid in a 2005 street racer, how an old PC shooter moved, how it felt to sit on a menu screen with the music playing.

Lately that means going deep. I rebuilt Most Wanted's handling in Luau from the game's own numbers, wrote 1998-style bunnyhop and air-strafe movement from scratch, and generated lo-fi music live in the browser with Web Audio. My main tools are Luau, Python and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus Three.js when a page needs real 3D.

I use AI tools like Claude every day to move faster, not to think for me: I break a problem into pieces, explain it clearly and check what comes back. The rule hasn't changed: ship it, then make it better.

“If it annoys me twice, I build a tool for it.”

Blokd!

Drag, drop, clear. A puzzle game you play on feel.

Type
Roblox game
Built with
Roblox · Luau
Strength
Combos
Status
Live now

What it is

You drag block pieces onto an 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Clear several in a row and you build a combo, and combos are where the big scores are.

Why I made it

I wanted a puzzle game you play on feel. Quick eyes, quick hands, and that “in the zone” rhythm, instead of sitting there thinking for ages.

Free code RebornWithRelease — redeem it in the shop for the Night City theme.

Inside

  • Solo beat your own best score
  • Co-op up to 4 friends share one board and take turns
  • Duels 1v1, and your clears send junk blocks to your rival
  • Chaos Mode & events frozen blocks, lava, conveyor belts, even bosses
  • Style 20 themed worlds, 30+ block skins, 60+ emotes
  • Progress daily quests, a season pass, a prestige shop and leaderboards

Goldngine

Moves like it's 1998.

Type
Roblox game
Built with
Roblox · Luau
Strength
Movement
Status
Demo

What it is

A free-for-all arena on Roblox where you bunnyhop, air strafe and rocket jump around the map. Everyone spawns with every weapon, ammo is scattered around, and when you die you're back in the fight in seconds. No timer, just drop in and play.

Why I made it

Roblox movement feels floaty next to the old PC shooters I grew up on. So I rebuilt that kind of movement from scratch in Luau, with the same speeds, friction and air control, and built a game around it.

Keyboard & mouse recommended — it's a demo, so expect changes.

Inside

  • Movement bunnyhop, air strafe, surf ramps, crouch jumps and ladders
  • 14 weapons crowbar, shotgun, crossbow, a guided rocket launcher and more
  • Free-for-all no time limit, 2 seconds of spawn protection
  • Stations health and armor chargers, plus medkits and batteries
  • Lobby a practice range with target dummies and no PvP
  • Extras gibs, explosions and an in-game developer console

Pistonart

Most Wanted (2005) handling, rebuilt on Roblox.

Type
Roblox tech demo
Built with
Roblox · Luau
Strength
Handling
Status
Test drive

What it is

You get a car, a whole city and a few tools, so players and developers can try out the driving system I built: how the car grips, slides, boosts, sounds and feels at 200 mph.

Why I made it

Most Roblox cars feel floaty because they lean on Roblox's built-in physics. I wanted the weight and drift of an old favourite, so I went to the source: I read the game's own handling numbers and rebuilt the whole car from scratch in Luau to match them.

Test placeholders from NFS: Most Wanted (2005) — not affiliated with EA or BMW, being replaced.

Inside

  • Physics my own suspension, tire grip and drift, running 120 times a second
  • Engine & gears engine sound that follows the revs, a real rev limiter, nitrous
  • Feel chase camera, tire smoke, skid marks, nitrous flames and sparks
  • Open world downtown, autumn hills, a port, a mountain pass and a highway, all built by code
  • Multiplayer race your friends around the city and the street circuit
  • Extras a street circuit with a lap timer and a car colour picker

04Web experimentHTML · CSS · JS · Web Audio

Oracle.exe

Greek myth × weirdcore.

The Oracle of Delphi lives inside a haunted old computer. Type any question and she answers with a cryptic prophecy. Ask the same thing twice in a day and you get the same answer, like a real oracle.

In ancient Greece people travelled for weeks to ask one question. I wanted that feeling in a browser tab, with eyes in the sky, pop-ups that ask if you're still there, and a start menu that won't let you shut down.

Ask her a question ↗

05Web experimentThree.js · WebGL · Web Audio

Golden Hour

My “now” page, built like a game's main menu.

A girl sits on a rooftop above a hazy golden city; the menu shows what I'm working on, my devlog and my setup.

I wanted a page that's nice to just sit on. The city and rooftop are real 3D, she's a 2D drawing sitting inside it, and the lo-fi radio is original music generated live in your browser. Everything in the menu updates from one small file.

06Desktop app · Open sourcePython · PyQt5

RBXL to Text

Lets AI read your Roblox game.

Roblox saves games in a file that AI tools can't read. This app opens that file and pulls out every script and object as plain text you can paste anywhere.

I wanted AI to help with my Roblox code, but it couldn't see inside my game. I built this for myself, it turned out useful, so I made it public. It exports to TXT, LUA or LUAU and works with Rojo.

See it on GitHub ↗
